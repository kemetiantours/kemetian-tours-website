Discover Siwa

An oasis out of time

Near the Libyan border, Siwa has kept its own language, crafts and way of life. Alexander the Great crossed the desert to consult its oracle; today travellers come for the silence, the springs and the sunsets over the salt lakes.

We pace the journey for comfort, with eco-lodges built from salt and mud, and a 4x4 day in the dunes.
