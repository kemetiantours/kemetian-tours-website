Good to know

Everything you need to know before you travel

Still have questions? We're happy to help.

Contact us
How do I book a tour package?

Book through our website, by phone or on WhatsApp. Choose your package, share traveller details and pay to confirm.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 2–3 months ahead, especially for peak season (October–April).

Will I receive a confirmation?

Yes — you'll get a confirmation email with your itinerary, payment receipt and contact details.

Can I book for a group?

Absolutely. We offer special rates and custom itineraries for groups. Contact us to arrange it.

What payment methods do you accept?

Major credit cards, bank transfers and online payment gateways.

Is my payment refundable if I cancel?

It depends on the package — some allow free cancellation within a set period, others carry a fee. See our terms for details.

Are there fees beyond the package price?

Any optional add-ons, taxes or service fees are always shown clearly before you confirm.

What if my refund is delayed?

Processing times vary by payment method. If it takes longer than expected, contact us and we'll follow up.

Do I need a visa for Egypt?

It depends on your nationality. We can advise you and help with the visa process if needed.

What documents should I bring?

A valid passport, your visa if required, and a copy of your booking confirmation.

Can you help with flights?

Yes, we can arrange flight bookings as part of your travel package.

Will I have a tour guide?

Yes — most of our packages include a professional local Egyptologist guide.

What if my flight is delayed?

Let us know right away and we'll adjust your itinerary and transfers.

Can I request special meals?

Of course. Tell us your dietary preferences and we'll arrange suitable meals.

Is there 24/7 support while travelling?

Yes, our team is available around the clock throughout your journey.
