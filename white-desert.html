The White Desert, chalk under the stars

Wind-carved white rock towers, a black volcanic desert and a night camping beneath the Milky Way.

Best timeOctober – April
Ideal stay2 days, 1 night
From CairoAbout 5 hours to Bahariya
Perfect forAdventurers, photographers
Discover The White Desert

Egypt's most surreal landscape

In the Farafra depression, wind and sand have carved the chalk into towers, mushrooms and shapes that glow white at sunset and silver under the moon. On the way, the Black Desert's volcanic hills and the sparkle of Crystal Mountain.

We travel by 4x4 with an experienced desert crew, and camp in comfort with a Bedouin dinner around the fire.
