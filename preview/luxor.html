Discover Luxor

The capital of the pharaohs

For centuries Thebes was the heart of the Egyptian empire. Today Luxor holds a third of the world's ancient monuments: the vast temple of Karnak, Luxor Temple glowing at night, and across the river the tombs of the Valley of the Kings.

We plan your days around the light and the heat — early mornings in the tombs, golden afternoons at the temples, and a felucca on the Nile at sunset.
