Discover Aswan

Egypt at its most relaxed

Aswan is where the Nile is at its most beautiful: wide, blue and dotted with granite boulders and white feluccas. It is the gateway to Nubia, with brightly painted villages and a warm, unhurried rhythm.

From here we reach Philae, the island temple of Isis, and Abu Simbel, where Ramses II carved four colossal statues into the mountain.
