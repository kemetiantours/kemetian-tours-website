Discover Faiyum

Cairo's desert escape

Just south-west of Cairo, Faiyum is Egypt's largest oasis — a green basin of lakes and fields surrounded by desert. At Wadi El Hitan, a UNESCO site, fossil whales lie in the sand where a sea once was.

Stay the night and the sky does the rest: far from city lights, the Milky Way rises over the dunes.
