Discover Cairo

The heartbeat of Egypt

Cairo is loud, warm and endlessly alive. Behind the traffic lie some of the finest Islamic monuments in the world, the churches of Coptic Cairo and the lanes of Khan el-Khalili, unchanged for centuries.

With a local guide the city opens up — the best koshary, the quietest courtyard, the café where Naguib Mahfouz used to write.
