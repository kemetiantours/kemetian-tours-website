Privacy Policy

How Kemetian Tours LLC collects, uses and protects your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Kemetian Tours collects when you visit kemetiantours.com, contact us or book a trip, and how we use and protect it.

Last updated: 13 November 2026

1. Who We Are

This website and our tours are operated by Kemetian Tours LLC, a limited liability company registered in Wyoming, USA. We are the controller of the personal information described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Information you give us

  • Booking and enquiry details: your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, country, travel dates, number of travellers, room preferences and any message you send us through our forms, by email or on WhatsApp.
  • Travel details needed to deliver your trip: for example passport details for domestic flights or hotel registration, dietary requirements or accessibility needs that you choose to share with us.
  • Payment records: invoices, amounts paid and the payment method used. We do not store full card numbers (see section 6).

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: pages visited, approximate location (country and city, derived from your IP address), device and browser type, and how you arrived at our site. This is collected through Google Analytics, subject to your cookie choice (see section 4).
  • Technical data: our hosting and content providers may log your IP address and browser information to deliver the website securely.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To answer your enquiries and prepare quotations (steps taken at your request before a contract).
  • To book and deliver your tour, including hotels, transport, flights, guides and entry tickets (performance of a contract).
  • To take payments, keep accounting records and meet tax and legal obligations (legal obligation).
  • To understand how our website is used and improve it (your consent, where required).
  • To protect our website and business against fraud and abuse (legitimate interests).

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for automated decision-making.

4. Cookies & Analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages and countries our visitors come from. Google Analytics sets cookies (such as _ga) and processes your IP address in shortened form. We do not use advertising or remarketing cookies.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, analytics cookies are only set after you click Accept in our cookie banner. Visitors elsewhere can decline at any time. You can change your choice whenever you like: Cookie settings.

We also store a few small preferences in your browser (for example your cookie choice and whether you closed a language banner). These are not shared with anyone.

Some pages include embedded YouTube videos, loaded from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com) and only after you choose to play them.

5. Who We Share It With

We share personal information only where needed to run our business and deliver your trip:

  • Travel suppliers in Egypt, such as hotels, Nile cruise operators, airlines, transport companies and licensed guides, so they can provide the services you booked.
  • Service providers who process data on our behalf: Web3Forms (delivery of website form submissions by email), Google (Analytics, Firebase website content, email), WhatsApp/Meta (if you message us there), GitHub (website hosting) and Cloudinary (images).
  • Payment providers and banks that process your payments.
  • Authorities where we are required to by law.

6. Payments

Payments are made by bank transfer, cash or through a third-party payment provider. When you pay by card online, your card details are entered on the payment provider's secure page and processed by them; Kemetian Tours never receives or stores your full card number or security code.

7. International Transfers

We are based in the United States and our tours take place in Egypt, and some of our service providers are located in other countries. Your information may therefore be transferred outside your country, including outside the EEA and UK. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, or on the transfer being necessary to perform the contract you made with us.

8. How Long We Keep It

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a booking: up to 2 years.
  • Booking, invoice and payment records: as long as required for tax and accounting purposes (normally up to 7 years).
  • Google Analytics data: 14 months.

9. Security

Our website is served only over encrypted HTTPS connections, access to our systems is restricted to authorised people, and we use reputable providers to store and process data. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and to respond promptly to any incident.

10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to its use, receive a copy in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time. Residents of the EEA, UK and Switzerland also have the right to complain to their local data protection authority (in Sweden, IMY). California residents have rights under the CCPA, including to know and delete their information; we do not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising.

To make a request, email Bookings@kemetiantours.com. We will reply within 30 days.

11. Children

Our website is not aimed at children. We collect information about children only when a parent or guardian provides it to book travel for them.

12. Changes & Contact

We may update this policy from time to time; the date at the top shows the latest version. For any question about this policy or your information, contact Kemetian Tours LLC, 30 N Gould St, Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA, or Bookings@kemetiantours.com.
