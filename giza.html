Discover Giza

Where every Egypt journey begins

On the edge of the Western Desert, the Giza Plateau holds the only surviving wonder of the ancient world. The pyramids of Khufu, Khafre and Menkaure rise above the city, guarded by the Great Sphinx.

We time your visit to avoid the crowds, take you inside a pyramid if you wish, and pair the plateau with the Grand Egyptian Museum, home to the full treasure of Tutankhamun.
